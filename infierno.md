# INFIERNO: Lava Dragon, Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

INFIERNO: Lava Dragon is made by Jaime Valencia, an individual developer. Contact: jaime.valencia.games@gmail.com.

This policy covers the test versions of the game on Google Play.

## What the game keeps on your device

- **Your progress:** Souls, run results, unlocks, missions and your dragon's name.
- **Your settings:** sound, language, breath aiming, and whether you are 18 or older. The game asks the year you were born on the first launch, keeps only that yes or no, and never keeps the year.
- **A game log:** a file of game events, kept on your device for testing.

## What the game sends

Nothing. The test versions have no account, no cloud save, no analytics, no ads and no purchases, so the game sends none of your data to us or to anyone else.

Google Play installs and updates the game, and Google may collect data under its own privacy policy, such as crash reports if you allow it in your phone's settings.

## Keeping your data safe

Everything stays in the game's own storage on your phone and is never transmitted, so your phone's own protection, such as its screen lock, keeps it safe.

## How long data is kept, and deleting it

The game keeps your data on your phone until you delete it. Removing the game deletes everything it kept. To delete it and keep the game, clear the game's storage in your phone's settings, under Apps.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

Later versions will add online features, such as cloud save, analytics and ads. Before any of them sends data, this policy will be updated and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
